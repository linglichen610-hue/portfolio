05 — The Common Thread05 — 底层逻辑

Different fields.
Same core skill.不同领域，
同一种底层能力。

Brand marketing. Guesthouse operations. Content creation. Long-term travel. On the surface, these look like completely different worlds. But they all trained the same set of capabilities. 品牌营销、民宿经营、内容创作、长期旅行。看起来完全不同的经历，实际上训练的是同一套底层能力。

🔍 Understand people🔍 理解人 💡 Understand needs💡 理解需求 📊 Read markets📊 理解市场 🔗 Integrate resources🔗 整合资源 ⚡ Create value⚡ 创造价值

A brand campaign and a guesthouse both run on the same logic: read the audience, identify what creates value, build the system, and make it work. Travel strips away assumptions and forces you to understand new markets from scratch. Content creation demands honest feedback from real people in real time. Every chapter was different. The underlying skill is the same. 07 — Now07 — 此刻

Work hard.
Go further. 认真工作，
也认真去远方。

Outside of work, I'm usually somewhere with bad signal and good views. I hike, I move, I show up in places where I don't speak the language yet — and I try to understand the people there before I take a single photo.

I'm drawn to ethnic cultures, local rituals, and the kind of knowledge that doesn't fit in a guidebook. Tibet wasn't just a backdrop — it was three years of learning how a community actually works.

After office life, after years of wandering — I'm ready to begin again. 工作之外，我通常在信号很差但风景很好的地方。我喜欢徒步、运动、去到那些语言还不通的地方——然后在拍第一张照片之前，先试着了解那里的人。

少数民族文化、当地的仪式与日常、那种写不进攻略的知识，是我真正感兴趣的东西。西藏对我来说不只是一个背景板，而是三年真实的生活与学习。

经历了办公室的日子，也经历了四处漂泊的岁月——现在，我已经准备好重新开始了。
